Why we name our partner

Anyone can say “we help the planet.” We'd rather show you exactly who plants the trees and exactly how many. Verdancy is proud to partner with Tree Nation, a global reforestation platform that funds certified tree-planting projects around the world.

Trees are planted where they do the most good: restoring forests, watersheds and habitat. They are funded the moment your first real payment clears, so the commitment is real and verifiable.
